# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: May 15, 2025**

Beetle VPN ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- **Device Information**: We collect your Android ID to uniquely identify your device for personalized services and support.
- **Personal Information**:
  - **Email Address**: Used for account registration, login, password resets, or communication with you.
  - **User ID**: Used to identify your account and provide a personalized experience within the app.
- **Usage Data**: We may collect anonymized data about how you use the app, such as frequency of use and feature preferences, to improve app performance.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain the app’s core functionality.
- To personalize your experience, such as remembering your preferences.
- To communicate with you, such as sending service-related notifications or responding to your inquiries.
- To analyze app usage to improve features and user experience.
- To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following cases:

- **Service Providers**: We may share data with third-party service providers (e.g., cloud storage or analytics services) to operate the app, but only to the extent necessary, and these providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- **Storage**: Your information is stored on secure servers, which may be located outside your country or region.
- **Security**: We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures (e.g., encryption, access controls) to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.
- **Retention**: We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the following rights:

- To access the personal information we hold about you.
- To request correction or deletion of your information.
- To object to or restrict our processing of your information.
- To request the transfer of your information to another service (data portability).

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Our app may include links or features from third-party services (e.g., analytics tools or ad networks). These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have mistakenly collected a child’s information, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via the app or email. The updated policy will take effect immediately upon posting.
